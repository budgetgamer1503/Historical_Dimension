<div><h1><span style="color: #c9a227;">Historical Dimension</span></h1></div>

Historical Dimension is a Minecraft Bedrock add-on built around three separate historical adventures: the Delhi Sultanate, New Kingdom Egypt, and Sengoku Period Japan.

Each adventure has its own dimension, story, enemies, structures, quests, and boss encounters. You access them through the Chronicle of History, then return to the Overworld when you are finished.

Each dimension unlocks as you gain experience: Delhi requires 10 XP levels, Egypt 20, and Japan 30. Your levels are only checked — they are never spent.

## <span style="color: #a63d40;">Delhi Sultanate: The Stolen Royal Seal</span>

Travel to medieval Delhi during a crisis inside the Sultanate.

The Royal Seal has disappeared and the city has been locked down while the palace searches for whoever took it. You will speak with characters around the city, follow the investigation, fight Commander Qadir's soldiers, defend the palace, and eventually confront Qadir himself.

The Delhi storyline has multiple quest stages and several scripted scenes as the situation develops.

Finish the story by returning the Royal Seal to Sultan Alauddin Khalji.

## <span style="color: #d49b28;">New Kingdom Egypt: The Black Sun Pyramid</span>

Enter an ancient Egyptian expedition centered around the Black Sun Pyramid.

Explore the ruins and pyramid while fighting mummified workers, Sand Skeletons, Sacred Scarabs, Jackal Stalkers, Royal Mummies, and other enemies. The pyramid's guardians are fast and relentless, so come prepared.

Several weapons and relics can be found during the expedition, including:

*   <span style="color: #d49b28;">Sun Khopesh</span>
*   <span style="color: #d49b28;">Spear of Horus</span>
*   <span style="color: #d49b28;">Bow of Neith</span>
*   <span style="color: #d49b28;">Staff of Anubis</span>
*   <span style="color: #d49b28;">Scarab Shield</span>

The expedition gradually takes you deeper into the pyramid and eventually toward the Pharaoh's Sanctum, where Kheper-Ra, the Devoured King, awaits.

Once every guardian of the pyramid has fallen, you will be offered passage back to the Overworld — no extra items needed. Egypt also includes guided objectives, checkpoints, and waypoints to help you move through the adventure.

## <span style="color: #b33939;">Sengoku Period Japan: Samurai Provinces</span>

Travel through a large Sengoku Japan dimension with villages, roads, forests, bandit areas, forts, and other structures spread across the region.

Progress through five main samurai bosses:

1.  <span style="color: #4f8a5b;">Jade Storm Ronin</span>
2.  <span style="color: #7566a8;">Tsukikage Ghost Samurai</span>
3.  <span style="color: #b33939;">Oni Blood Warlord</span>
4.  <span style="color: #397a9e;">Seiryu Dragon Daimyo</span>
5.  <span style="color: #555;">Kurogane Shogun</span>

These bosses have their own attacks and combat behavior rather than functioning as normal reskinned mobs. The encounters use attack telegraphs, multiple health phases, abilities, particles, animations, and boss cinematics.

You will also run into different types of bandits and villagers while exploring the provinces.

## <span style="color: #c9a227;">Getting started</span>

Craft the Chronicle of History and use it while you are in the Overworld.

From there, choose one of the three adventures:

*   <span style="color: #a63d40;">Delhi Sultanate: The Stolen Royal Seal</span> — 10 XP levels
*   <span style="color: #d49b28;">New Kingdom Egypt: The Black Sun Pyramid</span> — 20 XP levels
*   <span style="color: #b33939;">Sengoku Period Japan: Samurai Provinces</span> — 30 XP levels

The add-on keeps track of story progress so you can leave an adventure and continue later.
